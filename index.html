Why CouncilCompass exists

Student leadership rarely comes with a manual. Most people learn it mid-event — figuring out how to run a meeting, manage a team, or fix a plan that's falling apart, all while it's happening. CouncilCompass is a small collection of notes for that exact moment: practical, no-fluff guidance on leading teams, planning events, and making good decisions under pressure.
